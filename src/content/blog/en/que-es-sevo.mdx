**SEvO (Search Everywhere Optimization) is the umbrella strategy that unites SEO, AEO and GEO into one model: optimizing your content to be found and cited across classic search engines, answer engines and generative AI engines like ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity.** It is not a replacement for SEO; it is the framework that holds SEO, AEO and GEO together.

## Why did SEvO emerge?

Search fragmented. According to Semrush, more than **58% of consumers** now replace traditional searches with AI answers, and platforms like TikTok handle billions of queries a month. Optimizing only for Google is no longer enough: you must be **everywhere** people ask.

## SEvO = SEO + AEO + GEO

| Discipline | Where it acts | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| **SEO** | Google, Bing | Rank in the classic results |
| **AEO** | Featured snippets, "People Also Ask", voice | Be *the* direct answer |
| **GEO** | ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, AI Overviews | Be *cited* inside the AI's answer |
| **SEvO** | All three at once | Visibility across the whole search ecosystem |

## How do you optimize for SEvO?

Each front reinforces the others, and some tactics are measurably effective:

1. **Answer first.** Answer the question in the first 1-3 sentences (40-60 words). **44% of AI citations** come from the first 30% of the page (Semrush).
2. **Data and sources.** Adding statistics raises AI visibility by **+30% to +41%**, and citing authoritative sources by up to **+40%** (Princeton GEO study).
3. **Structured data.** JSON-LD (Organization, FAQPage, Article) so machines understand your content.
4. **Fast, static HTML.** About **69% of AI crawlers do not run JavaScript**, so content must live in the HTML (Vercel/MERJ, 2024).
5. **Freshness.** Content updated within two months gets up to **+28% more citations** (Semrush).

## Is SEvO an official term?

Not yet. As of 2026 there is **no academic consensus** standardizing it; other names coexist (GSO, AISO). But "Search Everywhere Optimization" is the umbrella term with the most traction, driven mainly by Semrush.

> In short: SEvO means moving from "showing up on Google" to "being the answer everywhere" — search engines, assistants and AIs — with fast, structured, data-backed content.
