**Core Web Vitals are three user-experience metrics Google uses as a ranking factor: LCP (≤2.5 s), INP (≤200 ms) and CLS (≤0.1).** A page passes only when 75% of real-user visits reach "Good" on all three.

## What are the current thresholds?

| Metric | Good | Needs improvement | Poor |
|---|---|---|---|
| LCP (loading) | ≤ 2.5 s | 2.5–4.0 s | > 4.0 s |
| INP (interactivity) | ≤ 200 ms | 200–500 ms | > 500 ms |
| CLS (stability) | ≤ 0.1 | 0.1–0.25 | > 0.25 |

**INP replaced FID in March 2024** as the official interactivity metric.

## How do you improve LCP?

The LCP element is usually the hero image or headline. To lower it:

1. Make the LCP element present in the initial HTML.
2. Use `fetchpriority="high"` and `loading="eager"` on the main image (never `lazy`).
3. Serve modern formats (WebP/AVIF) with a responsive `srcset`.

## How do you avoid layout shifts (CLS)?

Always reserve space: set `width` and `height` (or `aspect-ratio`) on images, iframes and ads, and preload fonts with `font-display: optional` or `swap` to avoid text reflow.
