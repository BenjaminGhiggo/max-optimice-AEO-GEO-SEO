**GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is the practice of optimizing your content so generative AI engines —ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews— cite it in their answers.** Unlike SEO, which aims for a position in the list of links, GEO aims for your brand to appear inside the answer the AI gives the user.

## Which factors increase AI citations?

A Princeton University study measured the impact of different tactics on visibility in generative engines:

- **Citing authoritative external sources**: up to **+40%** visibility (and up to +115% for lower-ranked content).
- **Adding statistics and hard numbers**: between **+30% and +41%**.
- **Including expert quotations**: around **+28%**.
- **Keyword stuffing**: **0% improvement**, and even a penalty on Perplexity.

## Where should the most important information go?

At the beginning. According to Semrush data, **44% of AI citations come from the first 30% of the page**. That is why you should answer the main question in the first sentences —the "answer-first" pattern— and leave context and examples for later.

## Does content freshness matter?

Yes. Content cited by AI engines is on average **25.7% fresher** than traditional search results, and pages updated within the last two months receive up to **28% more citations**. Keep visible update dates and review your content regularly.

> In short: evidence-rich content (statistics, quotes and sources), structured with the answer up front and kept fresh, is what works best for GEO today.

_Sources: Princeton GEO study (Aggarwal et al., 2023); Semrush, AI Search Statistics (2026)._
